Panorama Scholarly Group Editorial Directory

Type: Independent Academic Publishing Initiative

Model: Open Access

Focus: Editorial Transparency & Scholarly Communication

Panorama Scholarly Group (PSG) is an independent academic publishing initiative dedicated to supporting open scholarly communication through transparent editorial practice, responsible peer review, and distributed digital publishing workflows.

Our journal portfolio includes titles such as Global Review of Humanities, Arts, and Society (GRHAS), Health Nexus, Silence, and Journal of Social Cognition and Communication, alongside other publications in the humanities, social sciences, governance, interdisciplinary medical research, and emerging cross-disciplinary fields.

This directory presents the international network of editors, academic advisers, and scholarly contributors associated with our publishing activities. We regard transparent editorial governance, research integrity, and broad intellectual exchange as central elements of sustainable scholarly publishing.

PSG supports open access dissemination and maintains publication policies relating to peer review, publication ethics, correction practices, and editorial accountability. Selected journals within the group hold ISSNs assigned through the German ISSN Centre, while administrative and operational functions are coordinated through Hong Kong.
