Yangyi Ren is affiliated with the Department of Clinical Medicine, Second Clinical Medical College, Shaanxi University of Chinese Medicine, Xianyang, Shaanxi, China. Their research interests include Pharmacy of Chinese Materia Medica, Psychology, Adolescent Depression, Adolescent Mental Health, Social Cognition and Emotional and Interpersonal Functioning.

Within Panorama Scholarly Group, Yangyi Ren serves as Editor-in-Chief of *Journal of Social Cognition and Communication* and Assistant Editor of *Health Nexus*.
