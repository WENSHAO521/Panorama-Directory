Haipeng Zhao is affiliated with the School of Public Policy and Management, Tsinghua University, Beijing, China. Their research interests include Public Policy, Governance & Institutional Reform, Policy Implementation, State Capacity, Governance Systems and Social Sustainability.

Within Panorama Scholarly Group, Haipeng Zhao serves as Co-Editor-in-Chief of *Global Review of Humanities, Arts, & Society*, Chair of the Editorial Board of *PoliEcoM Administration Review* and Editorial Board Member of *Journal of Law, Psychology, and Communication Studies*.
