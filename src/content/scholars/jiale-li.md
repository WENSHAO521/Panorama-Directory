I currently serve as Professor of Vocal Music and Doctoral Supervisor at the International College of Arts and Music, Midwest University, USA. I am also a Postdoctoral Researcher in Music at Yonsei University, South Korea, and an External Master’s Supervisor at the School of Art, Shandong University. I focus intensively on vocal performance, vocal pedagogy, and music theory research, holding a doctoral degree from a U.S. university, possessing postdoctoral research experience, and accumulating abundant first-hand stage performance experience. I have long been active in international vocal exchanges. I have been invited to deliver vocal masterclasses and thematic lectures at numerous universities and have given more than ten solo recitals in Singapore, the United States and other countries, including a seven-day consecutive solo recital series in Singapore, where I performed 100 vocal works. My performance footprint covers major art venues at home and abroad. I have won multiple awards in professional music competitions worldwide, have long served as a judge for international music competitions, and have been committed to aesthetic education, earning multiple international Outstanding Instructor Awards. I have attained fruitful achievements in the fields of vocal performance, music education, academic research and international cultural exchange.
