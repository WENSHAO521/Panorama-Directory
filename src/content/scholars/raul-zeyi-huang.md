Raúl Zeyi Huang is affiliated with the International Finance Corporation (IFC), World Bank Group, Washington, DC, United States. Their research interests include International Law, Law and Society, International–Domestic Law Relations, Sustainable Development Law, International Judicial Politics, Development Finance, Global Economic Governance, International Political Economy and Infrastructure Investment.

Within Panorama Scholarly Group, Raúl Zeyi Huang serves as Editorial Board Member of *Rural Governance & Green Development*, Editorial Board Member of *Journal of Law, Psychology, and Communication Studies*, Editorial Board Member of *Journal of Social Cognition and Communication* and Editorial Board Member of *PoliEcoM Administration Review*.
